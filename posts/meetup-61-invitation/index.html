Software and Discovery

Hello, 世界!

We are happy, that Leipzig Gophers is frequently visited by people from outside Leipzig and hence we are moving, as an experiment, Meetup #61 out of Leipzig as well, specifically to charming Halle/Saale, where Klaustor Coworking provides an excellent space for meetups - see their event calendar for more interesting meetups.

We’ll have a short input presentation by Martin Czygan about software discovery and a Go tool that can help you find new and interesting tools and libraries. This will also be an opportinity to share and discuss contemporary software tools (inspired by a past user group event, #44).

Schedule

  • 18:45 Doors open
  • 19:00 Welcome
  • 19:10 Software Discovery and Tools
  • 19:45 Open discussion / Bar excursion

We’ll meet:

  • in person at Klaustor Coworking, at Peterssteinweg 14, 04107 Leipzig (OSM)

We are not sure, whether we will be able to provide an online stream, but if we do, it will be found under: https://meet.jit.si/LeipzigGophers61 (meetup.com does not let us change hybrid events after the fact, unfortunately).

Join us to discuss Go, software discovery in the age of AI and more. See you!
