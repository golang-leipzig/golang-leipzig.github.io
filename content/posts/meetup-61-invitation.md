## Software and Discovery

Hello, 世界!

We are happy, that Leipzig Gophers is frequently visited by people from outside Leipzig and hence
we are moving, as an experiment, [Meetup #61](https://www.meetup.com/leipzig-golang/events/312537729) out of Leipzig as well,
specifically to charming [Halle/Saale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halle_(Saale)), where [Klaustor
Coworking](https://klaustor-coworking.de/) provides an excellent space for meetups.

We'll have a short input presentation by [Martin
Czygan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/martin-czygan-58348842/) about software
discovery and a Go tool that can help you find new and interesting tools and
libraries. This will also be an opportinity to share and discuss contemporary
software tools (inspired by a past user group event,
[#44](https://golangleipzig.space/posts/meetup-44-wrapup/)).

### Schedule

* 18:45 Doors open
* 19:00 Welcome
* 19:10 Software Discovery and Tools
* 19:45 Open discussion / Bar excursion

We'll meet:

* in person at [Klaustor Coworking](https://klaustor-coworking.de/), at [Peterssteinweg 14, 04107 Leipzig](https://maps.app.goo.gl/facHqdPnZE2HwNNs8) ([OSM](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/6257928551))

We are not sure, whether we will be able to provide an online stream, but if we
do, it will be found under:
[https://meet.jit.si/LeipzigGophers61](https://meet.jit.si/LeipzigGophers61)
(meetup.com does not let us change hybrid events after the fact,
unfortunately).

Join us to discuss Go, software discovery in the age of AI and more. See you!
